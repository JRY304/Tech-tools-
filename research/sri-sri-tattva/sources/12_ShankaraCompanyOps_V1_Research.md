- Founding date, share capital, shareholders, directors, ATECO, revenue, employees: NOT FOUND. Searching the exact P.IVA "07293170481" gave zero relevant hits.
- Inference (low confidence): Florence P.IVAs run in sequence. 05767040487 is about 2006/07 and 06230360486 is about 2012/13, so 07293170481 points to a recent registration, roughly 2022 to 2025. A recently founded company would explain why aggregators have no balance sheet indexed yet.

Nothing found that links it as a subsidiary of Sriveda Sattva Pvt Ltd (the India-based owner of the Sri Sri Tattva brand, per the App Store developer name https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1497876885) or of a Dutch entity. The Italian srl could be one of those subsidiaries, but this is unconfirmed. srisritattva.eu presents itself as the official European importer of Sri Sri Tattva products.

## 2. Operations (from shankara.it snippets, may be dated)
- Shipping: GLS courier, with a tracking number sent at dispatch. Free shipping in Italy and San Marino above EUR 79 on the "Pagamenti e Spedizioni" page (https://www.shankara.it/pagamenti-spedizioni.html). The current header banner says EUR 69, so the threshold has changed over time. The standard Italian shipping fee was not captured in the snippets. Shipping abroad (Belgium/EU) costs and times: NOT FOUND in the snippets. A third-party review aggregator claims an average delivery time "within 5 days", but it is unclear which "Shankara" store that covers.
- Payments: PayPal and Postepay logos, card payments via the Banca Sella gateway, bank transfer (no surcharge; pay within 3 days and email the receipt; the order ships once the receipt arrives), and cash on delivery (contrassegno) with a EUR 2.50 surcharge, payable in cash only. No evidence found of Scalapay or Klarna.
- Returns (https://www.shankara.it/assistenza-clienti.html): withdrawal within 14 days, the customer pays return shipping by a tracked method, and the refund is issued within 7 days of receiving the goods. The "30 days" figure comes from a Trustpilot exchange in which the company said it was not responsible for a defect after 30 days. It is not the written policy.
- "Guadagna con noi" / "Opportunita": an affiliate programme. Any registered user gets a sidebar with an affiliate code, and the average commission is about 10% of sales generated (their example: EUR 10,000 sold earns EUR 1,000). Problems go to info@shankara.it, with a 24h response stated. Payout terms were not found. It is not presented as a job board.
- Wholesale/operators: an "Operatore olistico" category sells massage tables (folding, portable, budget to professional) and professional Ayurvedic massage oils (https://www.shankara.it/operatore-olistico.html). No separate trade price list was found.
- Consultations: an Ayurvedic consultations page and a "Tour del Medico Ayurvedico" page exist (https://www.shankara.it/tour-del-medico-ayurvedico.html). Pricing was NOT FOUND.
- NGO link: the link is not resolved. The Art of Living context suggests it points to an Art of Living / IAHV-type humanitarian body.
- Older contact: info@shankara.it (current contact is supporto@shankara.it).

It is an online store for yoga mats, accessories and clothing that also promotes Ayurveda, and it sells on Mercado Livre (Trotta says ML is still a small share of revenue). ## 5. Reviews
- Trustpilot (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/shankara.it): about 1,952 to 1,953 reviews. TrustScore is shown as 4.2 in one snapshot and 4 in another. About 82% are 5-star and about 15% are 4-star. Most indexed reviews date from 2013 to 2021, so the review flow appears to have slowed or stopped.
- Praise: fast shipping, good packaging, product quality, and long-time loyal customers.
- Complaints: late or missing deliveries (for example, an order placed 29/11/2020 had not arrived by 21/12/2020), customer service not answering, high prices, glitches on the website, and a mouldy oil pump that the company refused to handle after 30 days.
- Response pattern: short Italian thank-you replies to positive reviews and occasional replies to negative ones.
- Google reviews of the Scandicci site: NOT FOUND (it is unclear whether the site is a shop or only a warehouse).

## FLAGS
1. NO registry data found for Sri Sri Tattva Italy srl: founding date, capital, shareholders, directors, ATECO, revenue and employees are all unknown. A paid visura for P.IVA 07293170481 is required.
2. Ownership by Sriveda Sattva India, Shankara Europe BV (Weert NL) or individuals is UNVERIFIED. Shankara Europe BV "and its subsidiaries" is a plausible parent, but this is speculation.
3. The predecessor cooperative Shankara Italia S.C. (06230360486, same address) is effectively dormant: 2024 revenue about EUR 20k, a EUR 60k loss, and listed as inactive. Its revenue history from earlier years was not obtained.
4. Firenze Shankara Srl (05767040487, EUR 4.1M revenue) is a gemstone wholesaler and is UNRELATED. Do not attribute its revenue to shankara.it.
5. The Brazil EUR 3M claim is unsupported. Trotta's Yogateria (Rio) is small by third-party estimates (5 to 50 staff, revenue estimated at 0.5 to 1M), and it is a yoga-goods store, not a Sri Sri Tattva distributor.
6. The free-shipping threshold is inconsistent (EUR 69 on the live banner, EUR 79 on the older policy page). EU and Belgium shipping costs are unknown, so check the cart directly.
7. Written return policy is 14 days, not 30. The 30 days figure appears only in a Trustpilot reply.
8. Trustpilot reviews look mostly historical (2013 to 2021). Recent review activity is unverified.
9. All shankara.it data comes from search-engine snippets, which may show cached or old pages. Prices are snapshot-level only.
