## Interpretation
- "Amla" is far more plausible than "Alma": AMLA Natur is a well-known, near-Hamburg Ayurveda house with its own production and DACH distribution, which matches the director's description almost point by point. If a deal happened, it is most likely this company, but that is inference, not evidence.
- Context worth noting: Sri Sri Tattva's earlier German GmbH went insolvent in 2022. A buy-in to an established local producer would be a logical way to re-enter, which makes the story plausible but does not confirm it.
