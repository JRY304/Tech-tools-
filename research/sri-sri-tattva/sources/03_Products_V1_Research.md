## 5. Flags and uncertainty
1. Network allowlist blocked srisritattva.com, Amazon EU, Dookan, Jamoona, RASFF, Health Canada, regdatalab: all portfolio and presence data is from search snippets. SKU counts are estimates (+/- 30%).
2. Ghee / dairy-composite import status from India under Reg 2021/405 (and 2026/1189) NOT confirmed; decisive for ghee and Chyawanprash.
3. Sri Sri Tattva in RASFF: none found, but RASFF Window not queried directly.
4. Health Canada lead alert names "The Art of Living" brand Chandraprabha Vati; connection to current SST production unconfirmed but reputationally relevant.
5. SST sells mercury-containing Rasa Sindura product (Bruhat Vata Chintamani Rasa): any EU association with this line is a red flag for regulators and press.
6. Certification claims (WHO-GMP COPP, AYUSH Premium Mark, EU Organic) come from a trade-show profile; certificates not seen. ISO 22000:2008 reference is outdated.
7. [K]-marked legal points are from the researcher's knowledge (BELFRIT, French arrêté 2014, German BVL, THMPD practice, contaminant limits, cosmetics specifics) and were not re-verified this session.
8. Belgian label language: practice is FR + NL (+ DE for German-speaking region); exact legal requirement depends on product type (cosmetics: mandatory info in language(s) of the linguistic region; food: language(s) of the region where sold). Verify with FPS/AFSCA.
