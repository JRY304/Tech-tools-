## 4. Model analysis

### 4a. Options

| Model | How it works | Feasibility for a solo founder in BE | Key problems |
|-------|--------------|--------------------------------------|--------------|
| Marketplace (third-party sellers, commission) | Brands/resellers list, you take 10-20% | Low | Two-sided cold start; brands already sell via bol.com (43k partners), Amazon.com.be, Ecco Verde. Sellers have no reason to join a tiny BE-only marketplace. Platform liability under DSA and Belgian food supplement rules still lands partly on you. |
| Curated multi-brand retailer (buy stock) | Buy wholesale, resell with content and advice | Medium | Working capital, stock risk on short shelf-life herbals; each supplement must be notified in Belgium (FOODSUP) before sale; margin squeezed by free-shipping thresholds of EUR 35-50 set by competitors. |
| Dropship from brands/EU distributors | Shop takes orders, brand/distributor ships | Medium-High to start | Few Ayurveda brands offer dropship; lower margin (20-30%); slow, multi-parcel deliveries; you still carry consumer-law liability. Good for testing demand. |
| Hybrid: content/community-led niche shop + practitioner B2B | Small curated range in stock (fast movers) + dropship long tail + B2B to yoga studios/practitioners | Best fit | Leverages existing yoga/Art of Living community; B2B smooths volume. |

### 4b. Supplier terms (indicative)
- Independent health stores typically expect 45-50% retail margin, Net 30 terms; spas/studios 50-60% but small volumes. (vendor blog, indicative) https://wholesalehelper.io/blog/wholesale-pricing-for-health-wellness/
- Wholesale marketplaces (Faire, Ankorstore) carry several Ayurveda brands, with minimums around EUR 100-150 and up to 60-day terms. https://www.faire.com/brand/b_383wk6f26c ; https://www.faire.com/brand/b_cr7ozfwm70?signUp=1
- Amla Natur reseller terms not public; ask service@amla.de. https://www.shop-apotheke.com/ernaehrung/upmGAZXAT/classic-ayurveda-chyavanprash-amla-fruchtmus.htm

### 4c. Regulatory cost specific to Belgium
- Food supplements containing plants require prior notification to FPS Public Health via FOODSUP before being placed on the Belgian market (KB 2021). A fee (retributie) applies per notified product; amount not found in search. Many Indian SKUs (and some herbs) may need reformulation or be on the restricted plant list. bol.com also demands notification numbers for BE. https://www.health.belgium.be/sites/default/files/uploads/fields/fpshealth_theme_file/2023_10_18_foodsup_manual_fo_en_v10.pdf ; https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/offer/assortment-policy/daily-needs/health/ ; https://www.artixio.com/post/food-supplement-regulations-notification-belgium
- If the brand or its EU importer has already notified the product, the retailer can rely on that number; check per SKU.

### 4d. Conflict with the Sri Sri Tattva exclusive agency/distribution contract
- Commercial agency (Belgian Code of Economic Law Book X, Title 1): an agent owes loyalty during the contract and a written post-termination non-compete is valid only if limited to the type of transactions covered, max area and time per statute; it is unenforceable if the principal terminates without the agent's fault. Carrying competing Ayurveda brands (Classic Ayurveda, Maharishi, Himalaya) is likely a breach of an exclusivity/non-compete clause during the term unless the contract permits it in writing. https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-distribution/belgium ; https://www.legalmondo.com/?p=19821
- Exclusive distribution: under VBER 2022/720, an in-term non-compete is block-exempted only up to 5 years (tacit renewal allowed if the distributor can exit at reasonable notice and cost). A non-compete longer than 5 years or indefinite falls outside the safe harbour, but the clause is not automatically void; it needs individual Article 101 assessment. Practically: a 5-year non-compete is lawful and enforceable. https://cms.law/en/deu/legal-updates/10-key-aspects-of-the-revised-eu-competition-law-in-the-field-of-distribution-vber-vgl2 ; https://www.banning.nl/en/distribution-law-center/eu-competition-rules-for-distribution-agreements-5-non-compete-obligations
- Separate issue: a "Sri Sri Tattva Europe B.V." already appears as the manufacturer/seller on bol.com, so the brand already has EU distribution and likely sells into BE through bol. Exclusivity for Belgium must be checked against this. https://www.bol.com/nl/nl/p/sri-sri-tattva-triphala-tabletten-60-stuks/9300000057340393/
- Mitigation: negotiate an explicit carve-out listing permitted non-competing brands/categories (e.g. cosmetics, tea, tools, books) or obtain written consent; or keep the multi-brand shop in a separate entity only after legal advice (a separate entity controlled by the same person is usually still caught).

## 5. Verdict

- **Crowded, but fragmented.** About 25 online routes already sell Ayurveda to Belgians: Dutch specialist shops ship to BE at EUR 0-6 with free thresholds of EUR 45-75 in Dutch; Austrian niceshops (Ayurveda101, VitalAbo, Ecco Verde) cover FR/NL in many languages; generalists (H&B with 31 BE stores, Newpharma, Kazidomi, bol.com, Amazon) carry the hero products (ashwagandha, triphala, Pukka, Yogi, Khadi).
- **The gap is narrow and qualitative, not structural.** There is no strong Belgian-owned, bilingual (FR+NL), advice-led multi-brand Ayurveda shop. Belgian specialists found are tiny (Ananda Ayurveda, Mala India, Sanjivani), and Art of Vedas is the closest BE-facing specialist. A "bol.com of Ayurveda" (marketplace) is not realistic: brands already list on bol.com/Amazon, and Belgian demand is small (largest NL specialist ~332 Trustpilot reviews).
- **What could work:** a curated FR/NL shop with content, dosha guidance, practitioner and yoga-studio B2B, starting with dropship or small stock from 3-6 brands, after resolving the Sri Sri Tattva non-compete and checking FOODSUP status per SKU. Expect a lifestyle-scale business (tens to low hundreds of thousands EUR revenue) rather than a platform.

## FLAGS

1. **Raalte not confirmed.** Maharishi Ayurveda Europe B.V. shows addresses in Herkenbosch (company contact) and Roermond (KvK 67873251 via aggregator); the older Products Europe B.V. (KvK 13033652) is inactive. Check KvK directly.
2. **Sri Sri Tattva Europe B.V. already exists** as manufacturer/seller on bol.com (one snippet says Waalwijk, another listing says The Hague). Conflicts with any assumption of being the brand's only Belgian/Benelux channel; verify exclusivity scope.
3. **Non-compete risk is high.** Carrying Classic Ayurveda, AyuCare, Maharishi etc. while holding an exclusive Sri Sri Tattva agency/distribution contract likely breaches loyalty/non-compete clauses. VBER allows in-term non-competes up to 5 years. Get the contract reviewed by a Belgian distribution lawyer before any launch.
4. **FOODSUP notification required in Belgium** for each plant-based supplement before sale; fee per product not found. bol.com enforces this. Major hidden cost and time factor for a multi-brand Indian range.
5. **Blocked hosts:** WebFetch failed (DNS/allowlist) for www.ayurveda101.com, www.amla.com, www.pranayur.nl, www.ayurvedaspecialist.nl. The Agent web-fetch subagent was not available in this session. Product counts and BE shipping fees for Ayurveda101, amla.com, VitalAbo, Pranayur, maharishiayurveda.nl are unverified.
6. **No keyword volume or Google Trends data for Belgium** found; demand estimate rests on proxies (yoga studio counts, review counts). Run Google Trends / Keyword Planner before investing.
7. **Supplier margin figures (45-50%)** come from a software vendor blog; treat as indicative. Real Amla Natur and Sri Sri Tattva terms must be requested.
8. Some search summaries were generated by the search tool from snippets and could be imprecise (e.g. Holland & Barrett store counts dated Nov 2025; Art of Vedas country of incorporation not confirmed).
