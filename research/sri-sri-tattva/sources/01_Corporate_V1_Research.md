## 7. Org-structure weaknesses relevant to a freelance sales agent (analysis, partly inferred)

1. **The contracting entity is ambiguous.** At least 4 to 5 legal vehicles could plausibly sign with you in Europe: the NL B.V., the B.V.'s German branch, the new Frankfurt GmbH (2026), the Oppenau GmbH, plus the Indian parent. Insist on the exact entity, its registry number, and parent guarantees or payment terms. Check that entity's filed balance sheet, since the B.V. has only EUR 10k capital.
2. **Overlapping European units with conflicting directors and addresses** (Den Haag, Haarlem, Waalwijk, Frankfurt, Oppenau, Hamburg) point to an immature or reorganising European set-up. A brand-new GmbH in 2026 means its mandate, budget and authority are not yet proven.
3. **Decisions concentrate in the founder's family.** The MD is the guru's nephew and the board is long-tenured and closely held. Expect approvals to escalate to Bengaluru and possibly to be slow. Glassdoor reviews describe weak middle management.
4. **The workforce is shrinking** (down 15% year on year to Aug 2025, with layoff claims), and revenue has been flat against old targets. Budgets for agents and commissions may be cut or changed.
5. **Related-party complexity.** The listed subsidiary sells about 73% to the parent, and its CFO resigned in 2026 amid flagged audit-trail concerns. This is a governance yellow flag for counterparties.
6. **The channel depends on the AoL community.** Partners may find the company prioritises sales through the AoL network or volunteers over a commissioned agent's accounts. Watch for channel conflict with AoL centres, the srisritattva.eu webshop and Amazon.
7. **Regulatory exposure in the EU.** Ayurvedic supplements in the EU face novel-food, heavy-metal and claims rules (general EU knowledge, not company-specific). Product registration status per SKU is unknown.
